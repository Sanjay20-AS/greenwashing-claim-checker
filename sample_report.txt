2025 Sustainability Update

Environmental Performance and Climate

1. We reduced Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 32% from our
2020 baseline across our global manufacturing operations.

2. Our renewable electricity procurement reached 75% of electricity used
across our global operations in 2025. Scope 2 emissions are calculated using
the GHG Protocol.

3. We aim to achieve carbon neutrality across our operations by 2030.

4. Our climate targets are aligned with the Science Based Targets initiative.

5. Our environmental management system is certified to ISO 14001 at selected
manufacturing sites.

6. We are committed to a greener future and are continuously improving our
relationship with the planet.

7. Our products are eco-friendly and designed with sustainability in mind.

8. Our new beverage bottle uses 25% less plastic than the previous bottle
design.

9. We reduced water consumption by 18% in our manufacturing facilities
compared with 2022.

10. Our packaging is sustainable and better for the planet.

11. We achieved a 40% reduction in emissions.

12. We are working toward a cleaner and more responsible future.

13. We use 60% post-consumer recycled plastic in the packaging of our
Flagship Product A.

14. Our packaging is 100% recyclable.

15. We purchased renewable energy certificates to support our sustainability
journey.

16. We are proud to be an environmentally responsible company.

17. We have reduced operational waste by 22% during the reporting year.

18. Our environmental improvements demonstrate our commitment to a
sustainable future.

19. We have established a target to reduce Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions
by 50% by 2030 compared with our 2020 baseline.

20. We conducted independent limited assurance of selected greenhouse gas
emissions data for the reporting year.
